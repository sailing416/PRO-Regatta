NOR Publication Deadline — Plan Ahead
The NOR must be published far enough in advance to give competitors a fair and reasonable opportunity to enter. RRS Rule 89.2(a) requires the organizing authority to publish a NOR before the event. Sail Canada and World Sailing recommend a minimum of 6–8 weeks for major events and at least 2–4 weeks for club regattas. A late NOR creates real problems: competitors cannot arrange travel, boats, or crew; eligibility requirements may be unenforceable; entry fees may not be recoverable. If the NOR changes after publication, RRS Rule 89.2(b) controls what changes are permitted and how they must be communicated. Use this tool before you publish — not after.

NOR Requirements Guide
Notice of Race · RRS Rule 89.2 · Sail Canada Prescriptions
📄
The Notice of Race is the first official document of a regatta. Under RRS Rule 89.2(a), the organizing authority is required to publish a NOR. Use the AI Assistant on the right to ask any question about NOR requirements.
⚠️
The NOR is a binding document. If a NOR provision conflicts with the Sailing Instructions, the SIs take precedence — but only where they are permitted to change the NOR. Under Sail Canada Prescription 88.2, Sail Canada's Prescriptions to Part 5 and Rule 65.1 cannot be changed or deleted by the NOR or SIs under any circumstances.

★ Mandatory — Must Be in Every NOR RRS 89.2

Identity of the Organizing Authority ▾
  • ★Full name of the club or organization conducting the event RRS 89.1
  • ★Contact details for the organizing authority RRS 89.2
Event Dates and Location ▾
  • ★Dates of the event (racing days) RRS 89.2
  • ★Location — body of water, port, or sailing area RRS 89.2
  • ★Approximate scheduled number of races
Eligible Classes and Boats ▾
  • ★Which classes or divisions are eligible to enter RRS 89.2
  • ★Any restrictions on boat type, measurement, or certificate RRS 76
  • ★Whether a rating or handicap system will be used and which one RRS 89.2
Rules Governing the Event ▾
  • ★Statement that the event is governed by the Racing Rules of Sailing RRS 89.2
  • ★Any prescriptions of Sail Canada that apply RRS 88
  • ★Any class rules that apply RRS 89.2
  • ★Any other documents that govern the event (e.g. a measurement rule)
Entry Requirements ▾
  • ★Entry fee (if any) and payment details RRS 89.2
  • ★Entry closing date and how to enter RRS 89.2
  • ★Any eligibility conditions a competitor or boat must meet RRS 75, 76
  • ★Sail Canada Prescription Rule 46: the person in charge, if resident in Canada, must be a Sail Canada member through an affiliated club or Provincial Sailing Association Sail Canada Rule 46
Prizes and Scoring ▾
  • ★A description of prizes offered, or a statement that none are offered RRS 89.2
  • ★Scoring system — state if it is not the RRS Low Point Appendix A system RRS 89.2, App A
Protest Committee and Appeals ▾
  • ★If a no-appeal protest committee or international jury is appointed, this must be stated in the NOR or SIs RRS 70.5, 91
  • ★Sail Canada Prescription to Part 5: no fee may be charged for any protest committee procedure Sail Canada Part 5
  • ★If the right to appeal is to be denied, a no-appeal PC or IJ must be appointed and this must be clearly stated RRS 70.5

◦ Recommended — Best Practice Inclusions

  • ◦Approximate first warning signal time on each racing day
  • ◦Registration location, date, and time
  • ◦Liability disclaimer and insurance requirements RRS 4, 65.1
  • ◦Minimum third party liability insurance amount
  • ◦Safety requirements — PFD/lifejacket policy, VHF channel, safety equipment
  • ◦Whether Appendix P (Special Procedures for Rule 42) applies App P
  • ◦Whether Appendix T (Arbitration) applies App T
  • ◦Notice board location and official communication method RRS 89.2
  • ◦Advertising requirements or restrictions App G
  • ◦Sail Canada Prescription Appendix G: boats over 6m not in a recognized class racing under a measurement rule or handicap must carry sail numbers on mainsails, spinnakers, and overlapping headsails with LP over 130% Sail Canada App G
  • ◦Race office contact for questions (email/phone)
  • ◦Statement that Sailing Instructions will be available and when
  • ◦Any changes to Sail Canada Prescriptions — only permitted if a no-appeal PC or IJ is appointed Sail Canada 88.2

SI Requirements Guide
Sailing Instructions · RRS Rules 86, 89 · Sail Canada Prescriptions
📘
Sailing Instructions are the detailed operating rules for a specific regatta. They must be consistent with the NOR. Under RRS Rule 86.1, the SIs may only change rules that are listed as changeable.
⚠️
In Canada, Sail Canada Prescription 88.2 prohibits the SIs from changing the Sail Canada Prescriptions to Part 5 (protest procedures) and Rule 65.1 (liability) unless a no-appeal protest committee or international jury has been appointed.

★ Mandatory — Must Be in Every Set of SIs

Rules and Authority ▾
  • ★Statement that the event is governed by the Racing Rules of Sailing RRS 89.2
  • ★Any changes to the rules — each change must cite the rule being changed RRS 86.1
  • ★Any Sail Canada Prescriptions that are changed — only permitted if a no-appeal PC or IJ is in place Sail Canada 88.2
Communications and Signals ▾
  • ★Location of the official notice board RRS 89.2
  • ★VHF radio channel(s) used by the race committee
  • ★Racing signals — state if any visual signals are changed or supplemented RRS 25, App E
  • ★Time and date when the SIs can first be obtained RRS 89.2
Schedule and Course Area ▾
  • ★Scheduled time of first warning signal each day RRS 89.2
  • ★Description of the racing area (chart, coordinates, or written description)
  • ★Description of all possible courses, or how the course will be signalled RRS 89.2
  • ★The starting area and starting line
  • ★The finishing line
  • ★How marks are described and how they are to be left RRS 28
Racing and Timing Rules ▾
  • ★Time limits for the race — RRS Rule 35 applies unless changed RRS 35
  • ★Time limit for finishing after the first boat (target time) RRS 35
  • ★Recall procedure — individual recall (RRS 29.1) or general recall (RRS 29.2) RRS 29
  • ★Scoring system — if Low Point (App A) is used, this may be stated by reference RRS App A
  • ★How ties will be broken RRS App A8
  • ★Number of races scheduled and minimum number to constitute a series RRS App A9
  • ★Discards — how many and when they apply RRS App A9
Protests and Hearings ▾
  • ★Protest time limit — RRS Rule 61.3 requires a time to be fixed in the SIs RRS 61.3
  • ★Where protest forms are available and where to submit them RRS 61.3
  • ★Whether Appendix T (Arbitration) applies RRS App T
  • ★Sail Canada Prescription to Part 5: no fee may be charged for protest proceedings Sail Canada Part 5
  • ★Whether a no-appeal protest committee or international jury is in place RRS 70.5
  • ★Sail Canada Prescription 91(b): a 3-member IJ under rule N1.7 requires prior written approval from Sail Canada — appeals@sailing.ca Sail Canada 91(b)
Safety and Equipment ▾
  • ★Any specific safety equipment required to be on board RRS 4
  • ★PFD / lifejacket requirements — when they must be worn RRS 4
  • ★Distress signal and man-overboard procedures
Penalties ▾
  • ★Whether the Two-Turns penalty (RRS 44.1) or One-Turn penalty (RRS 44.2) applies RRS 44
  • ★Whether Appendix P (Special Procedures for Rule 42) applies RRS App P
  • ★Any other changes to penalty options must cite the rule being changed RRS 86.1

◦ Recommended — Best Practice Inclusions

  • ◦Clear description of how and when changes to the SIs will be posted RRS 89.2(b)
  • ◦Competitor check-in or registration requirements on race days
  • ◦Briefing time and whether briefings are mandatory
  • ◦Support boat identification and restrictions
  • ◦Equipment inspection details and timing
  • ◦Measurement procedures and certificate requirements
  • ◦Sail Canada Prescription Appendix G — sail number rules for non-recognized class boats Sail Canada App G
  • ◦Exclusion zone or restricted area description RRS 28
  • ◦GPS tracking device requirement (if applicable)
  • ◦Prize-giving ceremony time and location
  • ◦Environmental rules (no anchoring zones, waste disposal)
  • ◦Liability statement: under RRS 4, competitors are solely responsible for deciding to race RRS 4
  • ◦Sail Canada Prescription 65.1: a boat found to have broken a rule and caused damage is considered at fault Sail Canada 65.1
  • ◦How redress requests should be submitted and the time limit RRS 62

Rules Governing Race Organization

  • ▸RRS Rule 4 — Decision to Race: a boat is solely responsible for deciding whether to race
  • ▸RRS Rule 25 — Races to be Sailed and Signals
  • ▸RRS Rule 28 — Sailing the Course
  • ▸RRS Rule 29 — Recalls

  • ▸RRS Rule 61.3 — Protest Time Limit (must be fixed in the SIs)
  • ▸RRS Rule 62 — Redress
  • ▸RRS Rule 65.1 — Informing the Protestee and Others
  • ▸RRS Rule 70.5 — Decisions of Protest Committees Without Appeal
  • ▸RRS Rule 75 — Entering a Race
  • ▸RRS Rule 76 — Exclusion of Boats or Competitors
  • ▸RRS Rule 86.1 — Changes to the Racing Rules
  • ▸RRS Rule 88 — National Authority Prescriptions
  • ▸RRS Rule 89 — Organizing Authority; Races Governed by These Rules
  • ▸RRS Rule 91 — Protest Committee
  • ▸RRS Appendix A — Scoring
  • ▸RRS Appendix G — Identification on Sails
  • ▸RRS Appendix P — Special Procedures for Rule 42
  • ▸RRS Appendix T — Arbitration

Sail Canada Prescriptions — Summary

  • ▸Rule 46 — Person in Charge: If resident in Canada, the person in charge must be a Sail Canada member through an affiliated club or Provincial Sailing Association
  • ▸Part 5 Prescription: No fee may be charged for any protest committee procedure under Part 5 or Appendix T
  • ▸Rule 65.1: A boat found to have broken a rule and caused damage is considered at fault for the purposes of legal liability
  • ▸Rule 70.3(b): Sail Canada so prescribes — appeals may be made to Sail Canada
  • ▸Rule 88.2: The Prescriptions to Part 5 and Rule 65.1 cannot be changed or deleted. Other prescriptions may only be changed if a no-appeal PC or IJ is in place
  • ▸Rule 91(b): A 3-member IJ under rule N1.7 requires prior written approval from Sail Canada — appeals@sailing.ca
  • ▸Appendix G: Boats over 6m not in a World Sailing or recognized class racing under a measurement rule or handicap must carry sail numbers on mainsails, spinnakers, and overlapping headsails with LP over 130%
  • ▸Appendix R: Appeals submitted to appeals@sailing.ca. Sail Canada may delegate to a provincial appeals committee. Decisions of a provincial appeals committee may be further appealed to Sail Canada
